Verified semantics in those tests include:

- accepting a reaction atomically spends the Reaction economy bucket and required resource, recalculates the interrupted outcome, and a replay cannot double-spend;
- stale or invalid reaction responses leave authoritative state and payment unchanged;
- Ready-style stored invocation capture spends the Action at capture time, stores an authoritative artifact, and a matching later trigger atomically spends Reaction, executes the payload, removes the artifact, and rejects replay as a no-match;
- ignored, stale, and definition-revision-mismatched stored-invocation triggers do not mutate authority;
- stored-invocation cancellation is an authoritative artifact removal and the mechanics remain definition-name independent;
- deferred spell capture compiles `start-concentration` and `spawn-artifact` into the same Resolver transaction, then commits held concentration and the stored invocation together;
- concentration replacement expires the prior concentration group and damage checks use the deterministic concentration lifecycle semantics.
